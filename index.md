## 💡 What Makes electrum Different

Most Bitcoin wallets ask your computer to store a copy of every transaction ever made. That copy is enormous and can take a very long time to catch up. electrum takes a different approach: it connects to trusted servers that already hold that information, then shows you only what matters to your own wallet. The result is a program that is small, quick to install, and gentle on older hardware.

A few things you will notice right away:

- **Fast startup.** Open the wallet and it is ready to use in seconds, not hours.
- **Small footprint.** The software takes up very little disk space and runs comfortably on modest machines.
- **Clear overview.** Your balance, recent activity, and receiving address are all on one simple screen.
- **Built for regular people.** Menus are labeled in everyday language, and the default settings are sensible.
- **Open development.** The project is released under the MIT Licence and maintained by Thomas Voegtlin, with a public codebase you can inspect at any time.

Because it is written in Python and runs on the Qt interface, the application feels consistent and familiar, with standard windows, buttons, and dialogs rather than a command line.

## 🖥️ Opening electrum for the First Time

When you launch the application, you will be greeted by a setup window. It asks a small number of questions and then builds your first wallet. Take these steps in order:

1. **Choose to create a new wallet.** The startup screen offers this as the default option.
2. **Give the wallet a name.** Something like "Main" or "Everyday" is fine. The name is for your own reference.
3. **Pick the standard wallet type.** This is the usual choice for a personal wallet you control yourself.
4. **Let the software generate your recovery phrase.** electrum will display a series of ordinary words. This is the key to your money.
5. **Write the phrase down on paper.** Copy it by hand, in order, and store it somewhere private and physically secure. Do not photograph it, email it, or save it in a text file.
6. **Confirm the phrase.** The setup will ask you to re-enter the words to prove you recorded them correctly.
7. **Set a password if you wish.** A password protects the wallet file on this computer. It is optional but strongly recommended.

After the final step, the main window opens and your wallet is ready. A small status indicator at the bottom of the window shows the connection to the network; green means you are connected and up to date.

## 🔐 Keeping Your Funds Safe

Safety in electrum comes down to a handful of habits. None of them are difficult, but they matter more than any other part of using the software.

- **Your recovery phrase is everything.** Anyone who reads those words can spend your coins. Keep the paper copy away from cameras, roommates, and cloud backups.
- **Make a second copy.** Store it in a different physical location from the first, so a single fire or flood cannot destroy both.
- **Use a password.** It slows down anyone who gets access to your computer.
- **Check addresses carefully.** When receiving money, compare the first and last few characters of the address shown with what the sender expects.
- **Keep the software current.** Newer versions include fixes and improvements. Check the same download page periodically for updates.
- **Start small.** Send a small amount first, confirm it arrives, and then move larger amounts.

## 🧭 Everyday Use

Once your wallet is running, the main things you will do are receive, send, and review.

**Receiving.** Open the Receive tab to see an address you can share. You can create a fresh address for each payment, which makes it easier to tell transactions apart later. Copy the address with the copy button rather than retyping it by hand.

**Sending.** Open the Send tab, paste the recipient's address, enter an amount, and review the summary before confirming. The fee slider lets you choose between cheaper and faster confirmation; the default is a reasonable balance for most situations.

**Reviewing.** The History tab lists every transaction with its amount, direction, and confirmation count. A transaction with several confirmations is settled and very difficult to reverse.

**Backing up.** The wallet menu includes an option to display your recovery phrase again. Use it to double-check that your paper copy matches, then put it straight back into safe storage.

## 🛠️ If Something Does Not Work

Most first-time issues are simple to solve.

- **The status indicator stays red or grey.** Check your internet connection, then close and reopen the application. Network hiccups usually clear on their own.
- **The balance looks out of date.** Wait a moment for the wallet to sync, or restart it to force a fresh connection.
- **You forgot your password.** The password protects the file on this computer only. You can restore the wallet from your recovery phrase and set a new password.
- **You mistyped your recovery phrase during restore.** Words must match exactly and in order. Recheck each word against your written copy.
- **The window looks odd or too small.** Resize it by dragging the edges; the layout adjusts to fit.

## ❓ Common Questions

**Is electrum free?** Yes. It is open-source software under the MIT Licence, with no fees from the project itself. Normal Bitcoin network fees still apply when you send money.

**Do I need to download the blockchain?** No. The wallet connects to servers that already hold that data, which is why it starts so quickly.

**Can I use it on more than one computer?** Yes. Restore from your recovery phrase on the second machine. Only one copy should be open at a time to avoid confusion.

**What happens if my computer breaks?** As long as you have your written recovery phrase, you can restore your wallet on any other computer.

**Is my money stored inside the app?** Your coins live on the Bitcoin network. The application holds the keys that let you spend them, which is why the recovery phrase is so important.
